## About Me

I am a software specialist at HUS Helsinki University Hospital, working at the intersection of healthcare, information technology and clinical interoperability.

My professional background combines clinical nursing, software development, medical imaging systems, systems integration and strategic leadership. Having worked in healthcare since 2013, I bring both clinical understanding and technical expertise to complex healthcare technology environments.

I am particularly interested in developing reliable, secure and efficient information systems that support healthcare professionals and improve operational continuity.
